Privacy & Policy

This Privacy & Policy explains how Memorizer handles information and data when you use the application.

Why Ads?

Memorizer may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Advertising helps support the development, maintenance and future improvements of the application.

Depending on your location, you may be asked to provide consent for personalized advertising. You can manage your privacy choices through the consent options provided by the application.

Why AI?

Memorizer uses an external artificial intelligence service to evaluate answers when you choose to use the AI evaluation feature.

When AI evaluation is requested, the relevant question, saved answer and your answer may be sent to the AI service for the purpose of generating the evaluation.

Third-Party Services

Memorizer uses third-party services to provide specific features of the application.

Google AdMob

Google AdMob is used to display advertisements. Google may process certain information in accordance with its own privacy policies and applicable laws.

TypeSafe AI

TypeSafe AI is used to provide the AI answer evaluation feature. Information sent to the service is processed according to TypeSafe AI's policies.

Privacy

Memorizer does not require an account or registration. Information created by the user, such as questions, answers, categories and schedules, is stored locally on the device unless a feature explicitly requires communication with an external service.

Memorizer does not operate its own remote database for storing your personal application data.

Data and Information

The application may process information necessary to provide its features, including questions, answers, categories, votes, images and scheduling information.

When you use AI evaluation, the information required for that evaluation is transmitted to the external AI service.

Advertising providers may process advertising-related information according to their own policies and the consent choices available in your region.

Security

Memorizer takes reasonable measures to protect information stored locally by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including rights to access, delete or otherwise control certain information.

For questions regarding privacy or the processing of information, you can contact us using the contact information below.
