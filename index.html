1. Owner and Developer

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Memorizer manages user data.

Developer G Drako Interactive

Contact Email g.drako.interactive@gmail.com

For any privacy or data processing questions, you can contact the developer at the email address provided above.

2. General Information

Memorizer is an application for Android devices designed to help users study and memorize using questions, answers, categories, ratings, and review features.

The application does not require creating an account, nor does it require registration, name, email address, or password to be used.

Data created directly by the user within the application is normally stored locally on the device.

However, some features use third-party services, in particular:

  • TypeSafe AI for evaluating answers via artificial intelligence;
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob for advertising.

Using these features may involve transferring and processing certain data outside the device.

3. Data Stored Locally on the Device

Information created and used by the user within Memorizer may include:

  • questions;
  • answers;
  • categories;
  • ratings and scores;
  • images associated with questions;
  • application settings;
  • notification settings and review schedules;
  • other information necessary for the application to function.
Local Storage
This data is stored locally in the application's data area on the user's device.

G Drako Interactive does not maintain its own remote server or database to retain this personal user content.

Uninstalling the application or clearing application data through device settings may result in the deletion of locally stored data.

4. Answer Evaluation via Artificial Intelligence

Memorizer offers a feature that allows the use of an artificial intelligence service to evaluate an answer provided by the user.

When is AI used?
Information is transmitted to the AI service when the user chooses to use the AI evaluation feature.

To perform the evaluation, the application may send to TypeSafe AI:

  • the question;
  • the answer provided by the user;
  • the previously stored answer, used as a reference for evaluation;
  • instructions required for the artificial intelligence system to conduct the evaluation.

G Drako Interactive does not use its own artificial intelligence system to store or train models on user data.

According to TypeSafe AI's Privacy Policy, inputs sent to its services are not used to train or fine-tune artificial intelligence models.

However, TypeSafe AI may retain data for the period reasonably necessary to provide its services or for its commercial and legal purposes.

Personal Information
We recommend not including personal, sensitive, or confidential information in questions or answers that is not necessary to use the feature.

TypeSafe AI services are hosted in the United States. Therefore, when the user uses the AI evaluation feature, submitted data may be transferred to the United States for processing.

5. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Memorizer may use Google Mobile Ads / AdMob to display advertisements.

The relevant SDK may process technical and usage information regarding the device and application necessary to deliver, measure, and protect the advertising service.

Depending on consent settings and ad serving methods, such data may include advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, ad interaction data, and technical information used for ad delivery and measurement.

Advertising Consent
For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Memorizer may show a consent management interface for advertising and data processing according to applicable requirements.

The user can modify or revoke consent choices through privacy options made available within the application, where applicable.

Data processing methods by Google are governed by the privacy policies and terms applicable to Google services.

6. Data Not Collected Directly by Memorizer

Memorizer does not require:

  • account creation;
  • mandatory registration;
  • first and last name;
  • phone number;
  • home address;
  • password;
  • access to address book contacts;
  • GPS location.

Personal content created by the user, such as questions and answers, is not uploaded to a server owned by G Drako Interactive.

Data transfer to third-party services occurs when necessary for a feature used by the user, such as AI evaluation, or for advertising services operation.

7. Purposes of Processing

Data may be processed for the following purposes:

  • providing Memorizer features;
  • storing user-created content locally;
  • evaluating answers via the AI service requested by the user;
  • providing and displaying advertisements;
  • measuring and improving the operation of third-party services;
  • ensuring security and preventing abuse or illegal activities;
  • complying with legal obligations.

8. Legal Basis

Depending on the type of processing, the legal basis may include:

  • execution of the feature requested by the user;
  • user consent, when required by applicable law;
  • pursuit of legitimate interests, where permitted by applicable law;
  • compliance with legal obligations.

For advertising and consent-subjected processing, consent is requested through mechanisms provided by consent management services used in the application.

9. Data Retention

Data created by the user and stored locally remains on the device until the user deletes it, clears application data, or uninstalls the application, subject to device backup mechanisms managed by the OS or user.

Data transmitted to TypeSafe AI is subject to TypeSafe AI's retention policies.

Data processed by Google for advertising is subject to Google services' retention policies and periods.

G Drako Interactive does not independently store user questions, answers, or other created content on its own servers.

10. Security

G Drako Interactive takes reasonable measures to protect data handled by the application.

Data transmitted to online services used by the application is transmitted via secure network connections when supported by the respective service.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be considered completely secure.
